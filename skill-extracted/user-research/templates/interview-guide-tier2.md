# Interview Guide — Tier 2: Power Users
## (Senior ICs, Team Leads, Subject Matter Experts)

**Interview duration:** 45–60 min
**Interview type:** Discovery + Workflow Deep-dive
**Goal:** Understand the day-to-day reality of working in the problem space — specific workarounds, friction points, what "good" looks like from the trenches, and what they'd prioritize if given the chance.

**Before the interview:**
- [ ] Know their specific role and what processes they own or contribute to
- [ ] Review any past research notes on their team
- [ ] Prepare to go deep on workflows — be ready to ask "walk me through exactly how you do that"
- [ ] Don't bring solutions yet; this is a discovery session

---

## Opener (3 min)

> "Thanks for your time. I'm [name], working on research into [topic area]. I'm trying to understand what it's actually like to do [their work] day-to-day — the good parts and especially the frustrating ones. You're the expert here; I'm just trying to learn.

> I'd like to record this for notes — is that okay? It stays internal.

> There are no right or wrong answers. I'm not evaluating anything about how you work — I genuinely want to understand the reality."

---

## Section 1: Their World (8 min)

**Q1.** Walk me through what a typical week looks like for you. What are the main things you're working on?
- *Probe: How much of your time goes to each of those?*
- *Probe: What does a good week feel like vs. a bad week?*

**Q2.** Which parts of your work feel like they're running smoothly? What makes those work well?

**Q3.** Where do you spend time that you feel is wasted — work that shouldn't take as long as it does?
- *Probe: How long has it been like that? Have you raised it before?*

---

## Section 2: Workflow Deep-dive (25 min)

**Q4.** Walk me through the last time you had to [specific process related to research topic]. Take me through it step by step.
- *Probe: Where did you get stuck or have to wait for something?*
- *Probe: How long did each step take? Is that normal?*
- *Probe: What would you do differently if you could?*

**Q5.** Tell me about a time when [problem domain] really frustrated you. What happened?
- *Probe: How did you work around it?*
- *Probe: How often does this happen?*
- *Probe: What's the downstream impact when this breaks down?*

**Q6.** What tools or systems do you use for [topic area]? Which ones do you actually rely on vs. just tolerate?
- *Probe: What's missing from those tools that you wish existed?*
- *Probe: What have you built or hacked together yourself to fill the gap?*

**Q7.** If you had to hand off your work to someone new, what would they need to know that isn't written down anywhere?
- *Probe: Why isn't it written down?*
- *Probe: What tribal knowledge is carrying your team right now?*

**Q8.** What's one thing that would save you the most time if it were fixed or automated?
- *Probe: Why hasn't that been fixed yet?*
- *Probe: Have you proposed this before? What happened?*

**Q9.** Where do errors or mistakes most commonly happen in your workflow? What causes them?
- *Probe: What's the impact when they happen?*
- *Probe: What do you do to prevent or catch them?*

**Q10.** What decisions do you make regularly that you wish you had better data for?
- *Probe: What data do you actually have? Where does it come from?*
- *Probe: How do you make the decision when the data is missing or unclear?*

---

## Section 3: Reaction (8 min — use if validating a concept)

> "I'd like to share something we're thinking about. This is very early — I want your honest reaction, not validation."

**Q-R1.** [Show concept] — What's your gut reaction?
- *Probe: Does this solve the problem you described, or does it miss something?*

**Q-R2.** If this existed today, where would it fit into your workflow? Walk me through where you'd use it.
- *Probe: What would you need to trust it enough to actually rely on it?*

**Q-R3.** What would you change about this? What's missing?

---

## Closer (5 min)

**Q11.** Is there anything about how your work actually gets done that I should understand but haven't asked about?

**Q12.** Who else on your team or in adjacent teams would give me a really different view on this? Especially someone who's hit this problem harder than you have.

> "This has been incredibly helpful — much more specific than I could have gotten any other way. I'll make sure your perspective is represented in the findings."

---

## Post-Interview Checklist
- [ ] Notes captured within 30 min of session
- [ ] Workflow steps mapped (roughly)
- [ ] Top 3–5 friction points identified
- [ ] Workarounds documented
- [ ] Sentiment score assigned (−2 to +2)
- [ ] Urgency score assigned (1–5)
- [ ] Friction areas tagged (for heatmap)
- [ ] Any new process knowledge to add to the tribal knowledge list
