# Interview Guide — Tier 3: Cross-functional Partners
## (Adjacent Teams, Integration Points, Handoff Owners)

**Interview duration:** 30–40 min
**Interview type:** Discovery + Dependency Mapping
**Goal:** Understand how adjacent teams interact with the problem space, where handoffs break down, what dependencies exist, and what would make their relationship to the core team smoother.

**Before the interview:**
- [ ] Know what their team's primary responsibility is
- [ ] Know where their team touches or depends on the team at the center of the research
- [ ] Focus on handoffs, dependencies, and shared pain — not their deep internal workflow

---

## Opener (3 min)

> "Thanks for your time. I'm [name] and I'm doing research into [topic area] — specifically looking at how different teams interact with [core team or process]. I want to understand where things work well and where they break down from your team's perspective.

> This will be a listening session — I'm not evaluating anything, just trying to understand the dependencies and friction points that affect your team."

---

## Section 1: Their Relationship to the Problem (8 min)

**Q1.** How does your team interact with [core team / process / topic area] today? Walk me through a typical interaction.
- *Probe: How often does this happen?*
- *Probe: Who on your team is usually involved?*

**Q2.** What information, data, or outputs does your team depend on from [core team / process]? What do you provide back to them?
- *Probe: Is that information usually available when you need it? In the format you need?*

**Q3.** What parts of this interaction go smoothly? What makes those work?

---

## Section 2: Friction & Handoff Breakdown (15 min)

**Q4.** Tell me about a time when something broke down between your team and [core team / process]. What happened?
- *Probe: How did you find out it had broken down?*
- *Probe: What was the impact on your team's work?*
- *Probe: How did it get resolved — and how long did that take?*

**Q5.** What's the most common source of friction or delay at the handoff point between your team and [core team]?
- *Probe: Is this a one-off or does it happen regularly?*
- *Probe: Have you raised it? What happened when you did?*

**Q6.** What do you wish [core team] understood better about how your team works and what you need from them?
- *Probe: Why do you think that gap exists?*

**Q7.** If you could change one thing about how the two teams work together, what would it be?
- *Probe: What would that make possible for your team?*

---

## Section 3: Dependencies & Risk (8 min)

**Q8.** Are there decisions your team makes that depend on outputs or data from [core team / process]? What happens when those aren't available or are wrong?
- *Probe: Do you have a backup? What does that cost you?*

**Q9.** What's the one thing you're most worried about as [core team / topic area] changes or evolves?
- *Probe: What would you need to feel confident that change was being done right?*

---

## Closer (3 min)

**Q10.** Is there anything about how your team's work intersects with [topic area] that I should understand but haven't asked about?

**Q11.** Who else on your team or in adjacent teams should I talk to about this?

> "This is really useful context. I'll make sure cross-functional impact is captured in the research findings."

---

## Post-Interview Checklist
- [ ] Handoff points mapped
- [ ] Dependencies documented (what they need in / what they provide out)
- [ ] Friction at handoff points identified
- [ ] Sentiment score assigned
- [ ] Any cross-team systemic issues flagged
- [ ] New stakeholders to interview noted
