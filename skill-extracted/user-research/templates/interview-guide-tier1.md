# Interview Guide — Tier 1: Decision Makers
## (Executives, VPs, Directors, C-Suite)

**Interview duration:** 45–60 min
**Interview type:** Discovery + Strategic Context
**Goal:** Understand strategic priorities, budget realities, what success looks like for them, and what would make them champion or block this initiative.

**Before the interview:**
- [ ] Review their LinkedIn + recent public statements
- [ ] Know what their team owns and what they're responsible for
- [ ] Have 2–3 specific hypotheses you want to test
- [ ] Prepare to show a concept or framing in the Reaction section if applicable

---

## Opener (5 min)

> "Thanks for making time. I'm [name], and I'm leading our research into [topic area]. The goal today is to understand your perspective on [problem space] — how it affects your team, what's working, what isn't. There are no right or wrong answers here; I'm genuinely trying to learn.

> I'd like to record this session for note-taking purposes — is that okay? The recording stays internal and won't be shared outside the research team.

> I'll keep us on time. Any questions before we start?"

---

## Section 1: Context & Current Priorities (8 min)

**Q1.** What are your top two or three priorities for the next six months?
- *Probe: What does success look like for each of those?*
- *Probe: What's the biggest obstacle between here and there?*

**Q2.** How much of your team's time is currently spent on [problem domain] vs. where you'd like them to spend it?
- *Probe: What would you do with that reclaimed time if you had it?*

**Q3.** When you think about the tools and processes your team uses for [topic area] today — what's working? What isn't?
- *Probe: What has your team tried to fix this before? What happened?*

---

## Section 2: Pain Points & Decision Factors (20 min)

**Q4.** Walk me through a recent situation where [problem domain] slowed down a decision or caused a problem. What happened?
- *Probe: What data or information were you missing?*
- *Probe: How did it ultimately get resolved — and at what cost?*

**Q5.** If you could wave a magic wand and change one thing about how [topic area] works today, what would it be? Why that specifically?
- *Probe: What would be different for you personally if that were fixed?*
- *Probe: What would change for your team?*

**Q6.** What would make you confident enough to bet organizational resources on a solution in this space?
- *Probe: What evidence would you need to see?*
- *Probe: Who else in the organization would need to be on board?*

**Q7.** Are there internal champions or skeptics I should know about when thinking about change in this area?
- *Probe: What are the skeptics' main concerns? Are those legitimate?*
- *Probe: What's driving the champions' enthusiasm?*

**Q8.** What's your honest read on the organization's appetite for change in this area right now?
- *Probe: What's happened before when people tried to change [topic area]?*
- *Probe: What's different now, if anything?*

---

## Section 3: Reaction (8 min — use if validating a concept)

> "I'd like to share something we're thinking about and get your reaction. This is early-stage — I'm genuinely looking for honest pushback, not validation."

**Q-R1.** [Show concept / framing / prototype] — What's your first reaction?
- *Probe: What do you like about it? What concerns you?*

**Q-R2.** If this existed today, how would you use it? Who else on your team would use it?
- *Probe: What would need to be true for you to actually adopt this?*

**Q-R3.** What's missing from what I showed you? What would you add or change?

---

## Closer (5 min)

**Q9.** Is there anything important about [topic area] that I haven't asked about yet?

**Q10.** Who else should I talk to — inside or outside your team — who would give me a really different perspective on this?
- *Probe: Anyone who would strongly disagree with what you've said today?*

> "This has been really helpful. I'll share a summary of findings once I've completed all the interviews. Before I let you go — any questions for me?"

---

## Post-Interview Checklist
- [ ] Notes captured within 30 min of session
- [ ] 3–5 standout quotes identified and logged
- [ ] Sentiment score assigned (−2 to +2)
- [ ] Friction areas identified and logged
- [ ] Stakeholder segment updated (Champion / Pragmatist / Skeptic)
- [ ] New stakeholders to interview: noted
- [ ] Open questions added to tracker
