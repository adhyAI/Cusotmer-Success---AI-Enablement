## Tier 1 — Decision Makers (Executives, VPs, Directors)

*Tone: Respectful of their time. Short, direct, clear on value exchange.*

### Email

**Subject:** Quick research conversation — [Topic], 30 min

Hi [First Name],

I'm [Your Name], [Your Title]. I'm leading a research initiative to [one-sentence goal, e.g., "understand how our current data processes are affecting team velocity and decision quality"].

Given your role overseeing [their team/area], your perspective would be invaluable — particularly around [1–2 specific questions relevant to them, e.g., "what slows down your team's decision-making" or "where you see the biggest process gaps"].

Would you have 30 minutes in the next two weeks? I'll keep it focused and will share a summary of findings across the team once the research is complete.

[Calendar link] — or reply with times that work and I'll send an invite.

Thank you,
[Your Name]
[Your Title] | [Company]

## Tier 2 — Power Users (Senior ICs, Team Leads)

*Tone: Peer-to-peer. Emphasize that you want to understand their real day-to-day, not evaluate them.*

### Email

**Subject:** 30-min research chat — [Topic] workflow

Hi [First Name],

I'm [Your Name] and I'm doing research into [topic area] to understand what's actually working and what isn't from the people closest to the work.

Your role on [their team] — specifically [1–2 things they work on] — makes your perspective exactly what I'm looking for. I'm interested in the real day-to-day experience: the friction, the workarounds, what you'd fix if you could.

Would you be open to a 30-minute conversation? No prep needed, and nothing you share will be attributed to you by name in any report.

[Calendar link] or reply with availability.

Thanks,
[Your Name]

---

### Calendar Invite

**Title:** [Research Topic] — Workflow Deep-dive (45 min)

**Body:**

Thanks for making time. Here's what we'll cover:

**Agenda (45 min):**

- Wrap-up (10 min) — any questions, anyone else I should talk to

**This is not an evaluation.** I want to understand the reality of your work, not judge it. Everything is for internal research only.

## Tier 3 — Cross-functional Partners (Adjacent Teams)

*Tone: Collaborative. Frame it as understanding their needs, not just the core team's.*

### Email

**Subject:** Research chat — how [your team] and [their team] work together

Hi [First Name],

I'm [Your Name], working on research into [topic area]. I'm talking to teams across the org to understand how [core team / process] affects the people who depend on it — and where the handoffs or dependencies could work better.

Your team's perspective matters a lot here. I'd love to understand what you need from [core team], what's working, and where things break down.

Would you be open to a 30-minute conversation? Anything you share would go into findings about how the two teams work together, not individual feedback.

[Calendar link] or reply with times.

Thanks,
[Your Name]

---
